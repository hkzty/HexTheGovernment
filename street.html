Beat your own ghost.

Desktop WASD or arrows · Space handbrake · R reset · Esc pause. Pad stick, triggers, A handbrake. Phone on-screen pads.

Hex Street is a free browser street racer by HTG — Hex The Government. One night circuit, two rear-wheel-drive cars: a sky blue V6 sedan and an inline-six ute. Time attack against the ghost of your own best lap, with drift points for holding a slide. The circuit, both cars, the lighting and the engine sound are generated in code at runtime; nothing is downloaded and no real car names or badges are used. Also by HTG: Suit Purge.
