Tax Season is a free browser lane-runner shooter by HTG — Hex The Government. A crew marches up a five-lane boulevard and fires on its own; steering is the whole control. Gates add to the crew, double it, raise its fire rate or damage, or cut it down, and shooting a gate raises its number. Vaults pay out crew or a better weapon, lobbies keep spawning Politicians until they are broken, and a fork splits the road into a guarded path and a quiet one. Each bracket ends on a boss one step richer, bigger and uglier than the last: Millionaire, Billionaire, Trillionaire, Quadrillionaire and upward. Cash buys damage, fire rate, crew and income between brackets. Every model is built and lit in code at runtime; nothing is downloaded. Also by HTG: Suit Purge and Hex Street.
