About ABRAXAS

The project sits between depressive rap, distorted hooks, and a more code-styled visual identity. The sound stays emotional without getting soft, and heavy without turning into empty noise.

Released under the self-created label HTG, ABRAXAS is built for singles, visuals, merch drops, and live rollout all under one controlled page instead of being scattered across random dead links.
