About HTG

Hex The Government is a vessel, not a brand exercise. Heavy without turning into empty noise, honest without going soft. Music for the ones treading water while the people who flooded the room count the takings.

Singles, visuals, merch and live all run off one deck instead of rotting across a hundred dead links. Four names aboard so far — ABRAXAS out front, Stretty holding the hull, Justinn.clout and ciggyholster pulled up alongside.
